      <p>I am a Research Engineer at <a href="https://about.att.com/sites/labs">AT&amp;T Labs</a> in Bedminster, New Jersey. My work centers on wireless networking, mobile computing, and analytics for next-generation cellular networks.</p>
      <p>I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from <a href="https://www.utexas.edu/">The University of Texas at Austin</a> in 2025. At the <a href="https://wncg.org/">Wireless Networking and Communications Group</a>, I worked with <a href="https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~lili/">Prof. Lili Qiu</a> and <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/sanjay-shakkottai/home">Prof. Sanjay Shakkottai</a>.</p>
      <p>Before UT Austin, I earned an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from <a href="https://www.ucsd.edu/">UC San Diego</a>, where I worked with <a href="http://xyzhang.ucsd.edu/">Prof. Xinyu Zhang</a>, and a B.Eng. with honors in Network Engineering from <a href="https://en.uestc.edu.cn/">UESTC</a>.</p>
